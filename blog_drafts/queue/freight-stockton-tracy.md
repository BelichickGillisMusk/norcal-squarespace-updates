## 2026 clock, not a rumor

Most CA-registered freight units follow the DMV registration month and the six-month mark after that. Out-of-state units use the VIN-last-digit tables on CARB’s semi-annual page. Either way, **CTC-VIS is the deadline**, not a blog calendar.

Passing tests can be submitted up to 90 days before the deadline. That window exists so a fail can be repaired and retested before the hold. October 2027 adds quarterly OBD testing for OBD-equipped trucks. Opacity-path units stay semi-annual. Qualifying agricultural vehicles and qualifying CA-registered motorhomes stay annual. A road tractor on this corridor is not those exceptions unless the unit meets the published definition.

Official how-often page: [CARB emissions compliance testing requirements](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/clean-truck-check-emissions-compliance-testing-requirements).
