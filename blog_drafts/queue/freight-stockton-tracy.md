## 2026 clock, not a rumor

Most CA-registered freight units follow the DMV registration month and the six-month mark after that. Out-of-state units use the VIN-last-digit tables on CARB’s semi-annual page. Either way, **CTC-VIS is the deadline**, not a blog calendar.

Passing tests can be submitted up to 90 days before the deadline. That window exists so a fail can be repaired and retested before the hold. October 2027 adds quarterly OBD testing for OBD-equipped trucks. Opacity-path units stay semi-annual.
