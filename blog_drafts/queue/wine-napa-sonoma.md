# Wine-country support fleets — Napa and Sonoma

Vineyard support trucks in **Napa**, **St. Helena**, **Sonoma**, and **Santa Rosa** are not exempt because they park next to a tasting room. If the unit is over 14,000 lb GVWR and does not meet CARB’s agricultural-vehicle definition, it is on the Clean Truck Check clock.

NorCal CARB Mobile is a scheduled wine-country pin, not a daily Napa loop. Route page: [Napa–Sonoma CARB testing](/napa-sonoma-carb-testing) · [areas](/areas). Older explainer still live: [CARB Compliance in Napa Valley](/clean-truck-check-blog/carb-compliance-in-napa-valley-keeping-your-fleet-road-ready-in-wine-country).

**OBD $75** · **OVI $199** · Motorhome OBD **$99** · Motorhome OVI **$229**.

## Testing notes from St. Helena

April 23, 2026, same town, two different clocks:

- Commercial truck OBD, $75, 17-week / semi-annual follow-up.
- Motorhome OVI/OBD path, $75 that morning on the books, **not** the truck recurrence. Qualifying CA-registered recreational motorhomes stay **annual** (~300 days in field language). Do not clone the truck RRULE onto the MH event.

That is the wine-country oddity. Narrow roads and vineyard yards matter for routing. The compliance error that costs money is putting a motorhome on a truck calendar.

Ag vs haul for harvest trucks is a different post: [Harvest season: ag can stay annual, haul cannot](/blog/harvest-season-clean-truck-check-ag-haul).
