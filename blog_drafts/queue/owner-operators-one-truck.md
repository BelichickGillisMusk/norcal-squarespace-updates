# Clean Truck Check for owner-operators — one truck, one visit

If the truck is over 14,000 lb GVWR and it runs California public roads, Clean Truck Check applies. Fleet size is not the test. One truck still needs a passing result in CTC-VIS.

## What the law actually requires in 2026

Clean Truck Check is CARB heavy-duty periodic testing. It is not light-duty Smog Check.

- Most commercial trucks: **two tests per year** (semi-annual). Deadlines live in [CTC-VIS](https://cleantruckcheck.arb.ca.gov). CA-registered units usually track the DMV registration month and six months later. Out-of-state plates that operate in California still test.
- Passing results can be submitted up to **90 days before** the deadline. That window exists so a fail can be repaired and retested before a hold.
- Beginning **October 2027**, OBD-equipped vehicles move to **four tests per year**. Opacity-path units stay semi-annual. Qualifying agricultural vehicles and qualifying CA-registered recreational/emergency motorhomes stay **annual**. An owner-operator dump, mixer, or road tractor is not those exceptions unless the unit meets the published definition.

Official how-often page: [CARB emissions compliance testing requirements](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/clean-truck-check-emissions-compliance-testing-requirements).

## Which test the one truck needs

Confirm from the **engine** emissions label, not the door sticker year.

| Path | Typical unit | What happens on site | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| OBD | 2013+ diesel (2018+ alt-fuel) with a working OBD port | Plug-in scan, CTC-VIS upload | $75 |
| OVI | Pre-2013 / non-OBD diesel | SAE J1667 snap-accel opacity + visual | $199 |

Unsure: send year, make, and a photo of the engine label on the call. Do not guess OBD when the engine is 2011.
