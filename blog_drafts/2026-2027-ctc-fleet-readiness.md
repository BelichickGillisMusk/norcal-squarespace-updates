## What changes — and what does not

2026 is still a **semi-annual** year for most heavy-duty diesels over 14,000 lbs GVWR that operate in California, including out-of-state plates.

The 4x jump is narrower than most blogs claim:

- **Now through September 2027:** most trucks = **2 tests per year**. Agricultural vehicles and California recreational / emergency motorhomes = **1 test per year**.
- **Beginning October 2027:** **OBD-equipped** vehicles move to **4 tests per year**. Opacity-path (typically 2012-and-older diesel) stays semi-annual. Ag and CA recreational motorhomes stay annual.
- Source of truth for every VIN is the fleet’s **CTC-VIS** account, not a calendar guess. Passing tests may be submitted **up to 90 days before** a compliance deadline.

Who is in the program: nearly all diesel and alternative-fuel vehicles over **14,000 lbs GVWR** on California public roads, even if registered in another state.

What stops the truck: a **CA DMV registration hold**. A fine is a bill. A hold is a parked asset that still costs insurance, yard space, and missed loads.

Official cadence: [CARB emissions compliance testing requirements](https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/clean-truck-check-emissions-compliance-testing-requirements).

## Three steps that keep a VIN legal

Do these in order. A passing test with an unpaid CARB fee or a bad VIN in CTC-VIS still leaves you exposed.

**Step 1 — Portal and fees**

- Create / clean the CTC-VIS entity.
- Add every VIN exactly as DMV has it.
- Pay the **CARB annual compliance fee** in CTC-VIS (currently listed on our site as **$32 per vehicle**; confirm in the portal — CARB can change it).
- Watch the account weekly during open windows. CARB emails a monthly summary of VINs due in the next 90 days.

NorCal setup fee if you do not want to touch the portal: **$35** (CARB fee is separate). Full Care (deadline watch + reminders): **$80 per vehicle per year**, plus the test when we roll.

**Step 2 — Right test for the engine**

- **2013+ diesel / OBD-equipped:** OBD scan with a CARB-credentialed device. Mobile price **$75**.
- **2012-and-older / opacity path:** J1667 smoke + visual (OVI). Mobile price **$199**.
- **Motorhome:** OBD **$99** / OVI **$229**.
- Confirm on the truck. We charge the test performed.

**Step 3 — Hit the window, then submit**

- Test inside the 90-day window so a fail still leaves time to repair and retest.
- Only a **CARB-credentialed tester** can submit a passing result.
- Late paperwork is treated like a missed test.

Do not clear codes or pull batteries before an OBD scan. “Not Ready” is a failed window, not a maybe.
