## Section 2 — OBD vs OVI in one table (core of the post)

| | **OBD (HD-OBD scan)** | **OVI (opacity / visual)** |

## Section 3 — July / August timing without fake urgency

- Compliance deadlines for many CA-registered vehicles track **DMV registration expiration month** (Tables on CARB CTC pages) — **July and August reg months** mean real mid-year due dates for those units.
- Non-CA / special cases often use other keys (e.g. VIN digit rules) — still: **CTC-VIS is source of truth**.
- Semi-annual = second test often lands ~six months after the first cycle — summer is when a lot of fleets discover they’re behind.
- **Do not invent** a single statewide “July 31 hard stop” for every truck. Use: “check your account → book early enough for repairs if you fail.”

**Sales angle (honest):** Early booking protects route density and gives time if a truck needs work before retest.
