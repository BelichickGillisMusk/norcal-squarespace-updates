# Busy Corridor Week — Clean Truck Check Where the Trucks Already Sit

September stays busy along the NorCal corridor. Trucks don’t pause because the calendar says Friday — registration months still land, and downtime still costs. NorCal CARB Mobile’s answer is simple: Clean Truck Check at your yard. We come to you, we handle the paperwork side of the visit, and you stay focused on the route.

That field rhythm is what the maple syrup clipboard week looks like in practice — testers on asphalt, stickers on the path, no haul across town for a test slot. Appointments run seven days so a due date does not turn into a hold. First OBD test is $75; second truck on the same visit is $37.50.
