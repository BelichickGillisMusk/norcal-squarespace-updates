Clean Truck Check in Elk Grove: What Truckers and Ag Operators Need to Know

Published July 27, 2025 · Updated November 19, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile

Need a Clean Truck Check in Elk Grove? Most diesel trucks over 14,000 lbs test twice a year, trucks that qualify as agricultural vehicles under CARB's rules test once a year, and we can run either test at your Elk Grove ranch or yard.

What the Clean Truck Check asks of Elk Grove trucks

The Clean Truck Check (CARB's Heavy-Duty Inspection and Maintenance program) covers nearly all diesel and alternative-fuel vehicles over 14,000 lbs gross vehicle weight rating that run on California roads. For each truck you need to:

1. Report it in CTC-VIS, CARB's Clean Truck Check reporting system. 2. Pay the annual compliance fee in CTC-VIS. It's $32.13 per vehicle now, and $33.12 for deadlines on or after January 1, 2027. CARB takes debit, credit or eCheck, not paper checks. 3. Pass an emissions test from a CARB credentialed tester by each deadline.

Miss a deadline and CARB says the truck can face a DMV registration block and other enforcement.

Twice a year or once a year?

  • Freight, delivery, construction and most farm-support trucks: twice a year, every six months.
  • Vehicles that qualify as agricultural vehicles: once a year. CARB's definition is narrow: a vehicle owned or operated by a farming business and used only in agricultural operations, such as hauling farm products to the first point of processing. Check CARB's agricultural vehicle page or your CTC-VIS account before you assume a truck is annual.
  • Starting October 2027: OBD-equipped trucks move to four tests a year. Agricultural vehicles stay annual.

Which test each truck needs

It depends on the engine model year on the emission control label, not the year on the door.

  • OBD test ($75 per truck): 2013 and newer diesel engines, and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines.
  • OVI ($199): 2012 and older diesel engines get a smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions equipment.

Older reefers, flatbeds and haulers may still run 2012-or-older engines, so read the engine label before you book.

Testing at the ranch or yard

Harvest season is the wrong time to send a truck across town for a test. For Elk Grove farm and fleet trucks, we run a Cosumnes ag-yard test pass so the units get checked at the ranch or yard instead of leaving the property. The tester sends the passing result to CARB, so there's nothing for you to upload.

Questions and answers

Do ag trucks in Elk Grove need a Clean Truck Check? Yes, if they're non-gasoline and over 14,000 lbs GVWR. Trucks that meet CARB's agricultural vehicle definition test once a year instead of twice.

Can you test at my ranch? Yes. We test at your ranch, yard or packing site in Elk Grove.

What does it cost? OBD is $75 per truck and OVI is $199. Running 3 or more trucks? Call (916) 890-4427 for fleet rates.

Where do I find my deadline? In CTC-VIS. Your DMV registration month sets it.
