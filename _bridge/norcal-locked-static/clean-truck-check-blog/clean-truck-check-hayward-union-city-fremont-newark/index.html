Who needs it in the East Bay

The Clean Truck Check (CARB's Heavy-Duty Inspection and Maintenance program) covers nearly all diesel and alternative-fuel vehicles over 14,000 lbs gross vehicle weight rating that run on California roads. Along the I-880 corridor that includes distribution and last-mile trucks, drayage tractors, construction rigs, buses, and trucks registered in other states. CARB staff run roadside inspections outside ports and railyards, so port trucks get looked at.

Which test your truck needs

Read the engine model year on the emission control label, not the year on the door.

  • OBD test ($75 per truck): 2013 and newer diesel engines, and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines.
  • OVI ($199): 2012 and older diesel engines get a smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions equipment.
  • Motorhome ($99/$229): Call and we'll tell you which one fits.

How often you test

Most trucks test twice a year, every six months. For California-registered trucks, the dates follow your DMV registration month and the month six months later. Non-commercial motorhomes and agricultural vehicles test once a year. Starting October 2027, OBD-equipped trucks move to four tests a year. CTC-VIS, CARB's Clean Truck Check reporting system, shows your official dates.

You can submit a passing test up to 90 days before a deadline. That gives you room to fix a problem and retest before the date hits.
