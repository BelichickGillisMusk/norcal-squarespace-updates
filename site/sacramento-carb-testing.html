Sacramento fleets we test

Construction and aggregate haulers. Dumps, mixers, transfer trucks, and crew vehicles that live on Sacramento jobsites and at plants in Rancho Cordova, Elk Grove, and along the Highway 50 and I-80 corridors. We have written specifically about construction fleets in Sacramento and about a Rancho Cordova concrete fleet — those notes are linked below.

Agricultural and food-distribution fleets. Trucks that run produce, packing, and grocery distribution out of the metro and nearby Yolo County yards. If the vehicles qualify as agricultural under CARB’s definition they may be on the annual table rather than twice a year. The portal date for the VIN wins.

Owner-operators and small fleets. One truck or a handful, tested at the house, the rented yard, or the jobsite.

Diesel motorhomes and RVs. Class A, B, and C diesels over 14,000 lbs GVWR. Recreational CA-registered motorhomes are annual; commercial-use motorhomes follow the regular twice-a-year schedule. See motorhome testing.

Government and contractor rows. The Sacramento-area compliance guide already on the blog covers logistics, agriculture, and government heavy-duty diesels. We test the parked row rather than sending each vehicle to a station.

Pricing

  • HD-OBD periodic test — $75 per vehicle (2013+ diesel and 2018+ alt-fuel)
  • Mobile OVI — $199 per vehicle (2012 and older diesels: SAE J1667 smoke opacity plus visual inspection)
  • Motorhome and RV — $99 OBD / $229 OVI
  • NorCal Family — Full Care: $40 per year to us, plus CARB’s annual compliance fee paid through their portal, per vehicle. Testing is still at the standard rates per visit.

For a fleet of 3 or more trucks, call for fleet rates: (916) 890-4427.

Every test includes submission to CARB the same day and on-site proof of testing. We perform the test. CARB issues compliance. Deadlines and fines come from CARB, and a DMV registration hold does not clear in a set number of hours we can promise.

How a Sacramento visit is scheduled

Call (916) 890-4427 or use the contact form with the city, the yard, and how many vehicles will be there. If trucks are split between a Sacramento plant and an Elk Grove or West Sacramento lot, tell us both so we can build one morning instead of two trips.

Bring or photograph the emission control label if you want a preview of OBD versus Mobile OVI before we roll. We can also confirm on site. Agricultural vehicles and recreational motorhomes may be on CARB’s annual table; everyone else on this program is generally twice a year, lined up with the registration month and six months later. The portal date for that VIN is the one that counts.

Saturday appointments are part of the wider NorCal book when the route allows — ask when you call rather than treating Saturday as a printed guarantee.

FAQ

Q: Can you test my truck the same week in Sacramento?
A: Usually yes — Sacramento is our densest coverage area and we run routes through the metro weekly. Call (916) 890-4427 and we will give you the next open time.

Q: My registration is on hold at Sacramento DMV. What happens after the test?
A: We submit the test the same day. CARB issues compliance. How quickly a DMV hold clears is up to CARB and DMV, and we do not promise a clock for that. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: How much is a Clean Truck Check in Sacramento?
A: HD-OBD is $75 per vehicle and Mobile OVI is $199 per vehicle. Diesel motorhomes are $99 for OBD and $229 for OVI. Call (916) 890-4427.

Q: Do you also cover Roseville and Woodland?
A: Yes. Roseville, Rocklin, Davis, and Woodland are part of this Sacramento coverage, and the foothill towns have their own Valley and foothills route. Call (916) 890-4427.
