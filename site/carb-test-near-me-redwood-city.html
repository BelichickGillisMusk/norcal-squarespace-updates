Don't waste $8 diesel. We come to you.

We come to your yard, so you don't burn $8 diesel. A 30-mile round trip to a test station, at about 6 mpg, is about 5 gallons — roughly $40 at California diesel prices. Leave the truck at the Redwood City or South San Francisco yard and we handle the Clean Truck Check on site.

Who we test in Redwood City and on the 101 corridor

Diesel trucks, Peninsula warehouse and delivery fleets, and motorhomes that park in Redwood City, South San Francisco, and other Highway 101 yards such as San Mateo. We test at your yard, office, or jobsite. You do not have to burn a run up the freeway to a station.

On the Peninsula we can do a Peninsula 101 yard-side test stop in Redwood City or South San Francisco, so the truck stays at the yard. This is the Peninsula 101 corridor in San Mateo County, not the East Bay I-880 unit. The full Highway 101 write-up is CARB test across the Bay Area. The wider Bay picture is on Bay Area mobile CARB.
