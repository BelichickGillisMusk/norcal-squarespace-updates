Harvest windows and mixed yards

Lodi-area fleets often split vehicles between a vineyard or ranch gate and a warehouse closer to Stockton or Tracy. Say that when you book. We would rather build one San Joaquin day than split the same fleet across three visits.

Crush and harvest are not an exemption by themselves. If the truck is over 14,000 lbs GVWR and uses California public roads, Clean Truck Check applies. If you think the vehicle is agricultural under CARB’s definition, check the VIN in the portal.

Corridor, not a single downtown

Lodi, Linden, and Lockeford sit in agricultural country. Tracy, Lathrop, and Mountain House sit closer to logistics and I-205. Stockton ties them to the port. Those differences are why Lodi is its own corridor.

Official program details — who is in, who is out, twice a year versus annual — match the rest of the NorCal site and CARB’s published rules. Wine country follows the same published rules.

FAQ

Q: My trucks only run seasonally for harvest. Do they still need testing?
A: If they operate on California public roads over 14,000 lbs GVWR, yes — Clean Truck Check applies. Some agricultural vehicles follow CARB’s annual table. Call (916) 890-4427.

Q: Can you test multiple trucks in one Lodi or Stockton visit?
A: Yes. Fleet visits are our core business. For a fleet of 3 or more trucks, call (916) 890-4427 for fleet rates.

Q: How much is a Clean Truck Check in Lodi?
