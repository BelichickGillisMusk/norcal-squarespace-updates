Meet the Testers

Meet the Testers is the NorCal CARB Mobile about-us roster: seventeen fictional cartoon mascots, each with a short bio and a thumbnail that links out to YouTube. The real work is simple: NorCal CARB Mobile comes to your yard for a mobile Clean Truck Check, with HD-OBD at $75 per truck and Mobile OVI at $199 per truck. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

All of our testers are fictional cartoon mascots

Every member of the crew below is a fictional cartoon mascot, drawn as a clearly adult character. None of them is a real person, a real employee, or based on a real person. They are here to make each city we serve a little more fun and a little more local. The Clean Truck Check itself is done by NorCal CARB Mobile at your yard.

Don't waste $8 diesel. We come to you.

The crew has one motto: the truck stays put. A 30-mile round trip to a test station, at about 6 mpg, is about 5 gallons, roughly $40 at California diesel prices. Leave the truck at the yard. NorCal CARB Mobile comes to you.

Fresh from the CARB College of the Knuckle Draggers

The CARB College of the Knuckle Draggers is a made-up school with one class: show up at the yard, hook up, test, and submit the result to CARB. Think of the graduates as a shelf of soft, hand-stitched collectible dolls, each with a round face, a hometown, and an adopt-a-tester card instead of a diploma frame. Every mascot takes the same crew-bio yard roll call: a hometown, a featured test, a local habit, and a thumbnail that links out to YouTube.
