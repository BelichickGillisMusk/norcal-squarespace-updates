Communities on this route

The route runs along I-80 from Vacaville through Fairfield and Cordelia, north on SR-12 and SR-29 toward American Canyon and Napa, and south through Vallejo and Benicia toward the I-680 and I-580 junction.

“Travis AFB area” means civilian yards and parking around that part of Solano County. Military tactical vehicles are outside Clean Truck Check.

Napa and American Canyon also appear on Napa and Sonoma. If the yard is up-valley at a winery gate, start there. If it sits on I-80 or at the I-680 split, start here.

Built around Solano County truck routes

Fairfield sits where I-80, I-680, and SR-12 meet. Trucks in this area run toward Sacramento, toward the Bay, into Napa Valley, and toward the Delta. Many are parked at yards in Fairfield, Suisun City, Cordelia, and Vacaville.

Fairfield and Vacaville are Solano County cities on the I-80 corridor. Book Stockton docks on Stockton.

Industries on this spine are corridor trucking, warehouse and yard parking, waste and municipal contractors, food and beverage traffic toward Napa, and construction fleets that stage near the interchange instead of in downtown Oakland or Sacramento.

Instead of pulling a truck off the road to drive to a test station, tell us where it is parked and we will schedule the test there.

What we handle on site

We confirm the likely test before dispatch from the engine year on the emission control label and bring the right CARB-certified equipment.

  • HD-OBD — $75 per vehicle. Electronic Clean Truck Check testing for supported OBD-equipped heavy-duty vehicles (2013+ diesel and 2018+ alt-fuel).
  • Mobile OVI — $199 per vehicle. Smoke opacity (SAE J1667) plus visual inspection for older diesels that require the non-OBD procedure.
  • Motorhome — $99 OBD / $229 OVI.
  • Fleet and yard visits. Multiple vehicles can often be tested during one scheduled stop.

For a fleet of 3 or more trucks, call for fleet rates: (916) 890-4427.

If vehicles are parked at different locations along I-80, I-680, or SR-12, tell us the operating window so we can build the route correctly. We submit to CARB the same day.

Before we schedule

Have the city, vehicle count, engine years, and deadline ready. If one set of trucks sits in Vacaville and another in Benicia, that is still this corridor — tell us both yards so we can run I-80 and I-680 in one loop when the window allows.

Corridor carriers who stage near the interchange can start here. For a Port of Stockton dock, use Stockton. For a St. Helena winery gate, use Napa and Sonoma.

We submit every test to CARB the same day. Official program rules live on CARB’s Clean Truck Check pages. Deadlines and fines come from CARB.

Industries in one loop

Corridor trucking that stages overnight near I-80 and I-680, warehouse parking in Fairfield and Vacaville, construction fleets that would otherwise deadhead toward Sacramento or Oakland, and beverage or food traffic moving up SR-29 toward Napa all book along this route. Waste and municipal contractors with a Solano yard belong here too.

We are not a station. If the truck can sit still long enough for an OBD scan or a snap-acceleration opacity test, the yard you already have is the test site.

FAQ

Q: How much is a Clean Truck Check in Fairfield?

Q: Do you come to Vacaville, Vallejo, and Benicia too?
