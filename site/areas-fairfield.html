Built around Solano County truck routes

Fairfield sits where I-80, I-680 and SR-12 meet. Trucks in this area run toward Sacramento, the Bay Area, Napa Valley and the Delta, and many are parked at yards in Fairfield, Suisun City, Cordelia and Vacaville, or near Travis AFB.

Instead of pulling a truck off the road to drive to a test station, tell us where it is parked and we will schedule the test there.

Before we schedule

Have the city, vehicle count, engine years and deadline ready. If vehicles are parked at different locations along I-80, I-680 or SR-12, tell us the operating window so we can build the route correctly.
