West Sacramento yard testing

West Sacramento mobile Clean Truck Check: we test diesel trucks and motorhomes at your yard, jobsite, or office off I-80 and Highway 50.

West Sacramento calls are usually industrial and rental yards off I-80 and Highway 50. We park at the yard, test each truck there, and submit the result to CARB the same day.

Which test applies

2013 and newer engines: HD-OBD test, $75 per truck. 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines also test on OBD.

2012 and older engines: OVI smoke opacity test (SAE J1667), $199.

Engine model year on the emission control label decides the test, not the cab year. One visit can cover both.

Q: Which test does my truck need in West Sacramento, OBD or OVI?
A: It follows the engine model year on the emission control label, not the cab year. 2013 and newer diesel engines get the HD-OBD test at $75. 2012 and older engines get the OVI smoke opacity test at $199.
