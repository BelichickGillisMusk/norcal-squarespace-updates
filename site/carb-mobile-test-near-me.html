A plain-language map for the six names people type

People do not search “nine-county Bay Area.” They search the city on the side of the truck or the city where the yard is this morning.

If the pin is Hayward, start on the Hayward page and expect an I-880 industrial conversation — warehouses, Industrial Boulevard, and drayage toward the port.

If the pin is Fremont or San Leandro, it is the same I-880 unit. Newark and Union City ride along even though they were not on the original six-name card.

If the pin is Oakland, you are booking the hub city. Port-adjacent lots, jobsites, and warehouse rows are all normal. You will not get a street address for a counter.

If the pin is Richmond, say Richmond, not “Hayward/I-880.” The tester still comes from Oakland. The road is I-80 or I-580.

If the pin is Concord, say Concord or the inland East Bay. Highway 4 towns (Pittsburg, Antioch, Brentwood) are the same conversation and already have their own field notes.

Prices do not change between those pins. The grouping does, and that is what this directory is for: sending the request to the page that names the right freeway so the cities stay accurate.
