Mobile Clean Truck Check at a West Sacramento Yard

Published October 28, 2026 · Vin CA Diesel

West Sacramento keeps a lot of work trucks close to the river and the I-80 / US-50 corridor. When a fleet stays on the lot, Clean Truck Check should come to the yard — not the other way around.

Today NorCal CARB Mobile stopped at a West Sacramento equipment-rental yard for an on-site OBD Clean Truck Check.

West Sacramento equipment-rental yard

A West Sacramento rental, lease, parts, and service yard.

Hours posted on the glass were Monday–Friday, 8 a.m.–5 p.m.

What we saw on the lot

The yard runs white hydro-vac / Hurricane Rebel Vac trucks. One tank carried unit V1413. Those are the kind of heavy diesels that sit in a rental and service lane all day — and still need Clean Truck Check on California’s schedule.

Why West Sac fleets book on-site Clean Truck Check

Driving a hydro-vac or service truck out of West Sacramento to a fixed station costs fuel, driver time, and a slot in the workday. An OBD Clean Truck Check at the yard keeps the truck where the work is.

NorCal CARB Mobile covers the Sacramento corridor, including West Sacramento. See our Sacramento Clean Truck Check page and services. For program basics, read Clean Truck Check facts.
