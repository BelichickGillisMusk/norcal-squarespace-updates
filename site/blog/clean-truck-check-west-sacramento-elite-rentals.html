Mobile Clean Truck Check on Channel Drive, West Sacramento

Published October 28, 2026 · Vin CA Diesel

West Sacramento keeps a lot of work trucks close to the river and the I-80 / US-50 corridor. When a fleet stays on the lot, Clean Truck Check should come to the yard — not the other way around.

Today NorCal CARB Mobile stopped at Elite Rentals on Channel Drive for an on-site OBD Clean Truck Check.

What we saw on the lot

The yard runs white hydro-vac / Hurricane Rebel Vac trucks. One tank carried unit V1413. Those are the kind of heavy diesels that sit in a rental and service lane all day — and still need Clean Truck Check on California’s schedule.

Why West Sac fleets book on-site Clean Truck Check

Driving a hydro-vac or service truck off the Channel Drive corridor to a fixed station costs fuel, driver time, and a slot in the workday. An OBD Clean Truck Check at the yard keeps the truck where the work is.
