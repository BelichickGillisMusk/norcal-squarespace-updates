Meet the Tester Crew: Graduates of the CARB College of the Knuckle Draggers

Published November 22, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile

The NorCal CARB Mobile tester crew is a set of cartoon mascots, one for each city page, and every one of them is made up. The real work is simple: NorCal CARB Mobile comes to your yard for a mobile Clean Truck Check, with HD-OBD at $75 per truck and Mobile OVI at $199 per truck. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

All of our testers are fictional cartoon mascots

Every member of the crew below is a fictional cartoon mascot. None of them is a real person, a real employee, or based on a real person. They are here to make each city page a little more fun and a little more local. The Clean Truck Check itself is done by NorCal CARB Mobile at your yard.

Don't waste $8 diesel. We come to you.

The crew has one motto: the truck stays put. A 30-mile round trip to a test station, at about 6 mpg, is about 5 gallons, roughly $40 at California diesel prices. Leave the truck at the yard. NorCal CARB Mobile comes to you.

Fresh from the CARB College of the Knuckle Draggers

The CARB College of the Knuckle Draggers is a made-up school with one class: show up at the yard, hook up, test, and submit the result to CARB. Think of the graduates as a shelf of soft, hand-stitched collectible dolls, each with a round face, a hometown, and an adopt-a-tester card instead of a diploma frame. Every graduate takes the same yard-row diploma walk: we come to your yard, test each truck where it sits, and submit the result to CARB.
