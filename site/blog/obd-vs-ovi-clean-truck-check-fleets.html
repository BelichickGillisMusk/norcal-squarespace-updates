What Clean Truck Check is testing for

CARB Clean Truck Check is periodic heavy-duty emissions compliance for diesel vehicles in the program — not light-duty Smog Check. In 2026, most non-exempt vehicles still run on a semi-annual testing cadence (twice a year). You can often test up to about 90 days before a compliance deadline, but the source of truth for each VIN is the fleet’s CTC-VIS account, not a blog table.

Looking ahead, OBD-equipped vehicles are expected to move toward more frequent testing in later years. Treat that as “coming,” not the only story for today’s booking.

Why fleets mix them up

FAQ

Which Clean Truck Check test does my truck need, OBD or OVI?

It depends on the engine model year, not the truck model year. Diesel engines from 2013 and newer get an OBD scan. Diesel engines from 2012 and older get an OVI, which is a smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions control equipment.

How do I find my engine model year?

Check the emissions control label on the engine. Most trucks have an engine one year older than the truck, so the label is the safest way to know which test applies.

What if my on-road truck has an off-road engine?

Off-road engines have no OBD requirement under Clean Truck Check, so those vehicles get the OVI (smoke opacity and visual inspection) no matter the engine model year.

How early can I test before my deadline?

CARB lets a passing test be submitted up to 90 days before the vehicle's compliance deadline. Your CTC-VIS account shows each vehicle's due date.
