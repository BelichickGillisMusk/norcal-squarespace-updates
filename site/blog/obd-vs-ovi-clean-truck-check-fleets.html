What Clean Truck Check is testing for

CARB Clean Truck Check is periodic heavy-duty emissions compliance for diesel vehicles in the program — not light-duty Smog Check. In 2026, most non-exempt vehicles still run on a semi-annual testing cadence (twice a year). You can often test up to about 90 days before a compliance deadline, but the source of truth for each VIN is the fleet's CTC-VIS account, not a generic calendar.

Looking ahead, OBD-equipped vehicles are expected to move toward more frequent testing in later years. Treat that as coming, not the only story for today's booking.

If you are due, call (916) 890-4427 and we will confirm the test type before we roll. Official program details always live with CARB / CTC-VIS.

OBD vs OVI — the plain rule

CARB publishes the diesel cutoff by engine model year: 2013 and newer take an OBD scan; 2012 and older take OVI (smoke opacity plus a visual inspection of the emissions control equipment). Confirm that year from the engine emissions label when you book, and tell us the engine family if you have it. The engine year is often older than the truck year, so the label is the safest way to know which test applies.

Off-road engines in on-road trucks have no OBD requirement under Clean Truck Check, so those vehicles get the OVI no matter the engine model year. 2018-and-newer alt-fuel engines are on the OBD path.

We bring the right kit to your yard. A mixed fleet can need both tests in one visit. The $199 smoke-opacity visit is covered on the mobile OVI test page.

Why the two tests get mixed up:

  • Both show up under "Clean Truck Check" language on the same account
  • Shop quotes often lump "emissions" without saying OBD vs opacity equipment
  • Deadline math uses VIN plus account status — not a single calendar for every truck

FAQ

Q: How much does a Clean Truck Check cost?
A: Our mobile test prices are $75 per truck for an OBD scan and $199 per truck for an OVI smoke opacity and visual inspection, done at your yard. Motorhomes are $99 OBD / $229 OVI. Fleet of 3 or more trucks? Call for fleet rates: (916) 890-4427. CARB also charges a separate annual compliance fee, which you pay in your CTC-VIS account.

Q: How do I register for the Clean Truck Check program?
A: Register in CARB's CTC-VIS system: create an account or log in, then add each vehicle. That portal is also where due dates and compliance certificates show up. Testing is a separate step — call (916) 890-4427 and we will come to your Bay Area yard.

Q: How do I know if my vehicle has an OBD system?
A: Check the engine emissions label, not the truck model year. Diesel engines from 2013 and newer get an OBD scan. Diesel engines from 2012 and older, and off-road engines in on-road trucks, get an OVI. 2018-and-newer alt-fuel engines are on the OBD path. Call (916) 890-4427 if you want help reading the label before we come out.

Q: Where do I get emissions compliance testing done?
A: We come to your yard, office, or jobsite in the Bay Area, including Alameda, Hayward, and the rest of our East Bay and South Bay coverage. You do not have to drive the truck to a station. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: How often do I have to do emissions testing and when?
A: In 2026, most vehicles in the program still test twice a year. You can often test up to about 90 days before a compliance deadline. The source of truth for each VIN is your CTC-VIS account. Call (916) 890-4427 when you are ready to book.
