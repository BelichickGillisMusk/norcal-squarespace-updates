San Francisco Clean Truck Check Without Leaving Your Yard

Published November 22, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile

In San Francisco, a tight yard or a hard-won street stall is a reason to leave the truck where it sits — NorCal CARB Mobile comes to you for a mobile Clean Truck Check, with HD-OBD at $75 per truck and Mobile OVI at $199 per truck. Call (916) 890-4427 to book a yard visit.

Don't waste $8 diesel. We come to you.

Driving a truck out of San Francisco for a Clean Truck Check burns fuel you do not need to spend. A 30-mile round trip, at about 6 mpg, is about 5 gallons — about $40 at California diesel prices. Leaving the city can also mean a Highway 101 run or a Bay Bridge crossing, plus a toll on the way back in. Leave the truck in the yard. NorCal CARB Mobile comes to you.

Tight yards and street parking in San Francisco

Many San Francisco trucks sit in compact lots, alley docks, or a single street stall. Once that truck pulls out, the space may be gone when you get back. Construction, warehouse, and delivery fleets already fight for that stall; a tester run should not be the reason it is empty.

We treat a San Francisco visit as a tight-yard stall-keep test stop: the truck stays in its stall, and we run the Clean Truck Check there. If a vehicle is due, or already on a DMV registration hold, leaving the city does not make the hold clear faster — that timing is up to CARB and DMV. What we do is test at the yard and submit the result to CARB.

Coverage for San Francisco, including how a visit is booked, is on the San Francisco mobile CARB page. Peninsula yards in Redwood City and South San Francisco can use Redwood City and Peninsula mobile CARB. The wider map is on service areas.

Which test your truck needs

2013-and-newer diesel engines, and 2018-and-newer alt-fuel engines, get an HD-OBD scan for $75 per truck. Older diesel engines and non-OBD engines get a Mobile OVI smoke opacity and visual inspection for $199 per truck. Motorhomes are $99 OBD / $229 OVI. Go by the engine model year on the emissions label, not the truck year, if the two differ.

A mixed fleet can need both tests in one visit. The plain comparison is on OBD vs OVI for fleets.

FAQs

Q: Do I have to drive my truck out of San Francisco for a Clean Truck Check?
A: No. NorCal CARB Mobile comes to your yard, shop, office, or jobsite in San Francisco. The truck stays in its stall. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: How much does a Clean Truck Check cost in San Francisco?
A: NorCal CARB Mobile charges HD-OBD $75 per truck for 2013+ diesel and 2018+ alt-fuel engines, Mobile OVI $199 per truck for pre-2013 and non-OBD engines, and $99 OBD / $229 OVI for motorhomes. That is the tester price for a mobile test at your yard. Q: Which test does my truck need, OBD or OVI?
A: HD-OBD is $75 for 2013+ diesel and 2018+ alt-fuel engines. Mobile OVI is $199 for pre-2013 and non-OBD engines. Motorhomes are $99 OBD / $229 OVI. Check the engine emissions label for the engine model year, then book. Call (916) 890-4427.
