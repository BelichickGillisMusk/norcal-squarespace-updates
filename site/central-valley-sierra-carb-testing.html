Fleet and bus visits

Multiple vehicles can often be tested during one scheduled yard or parking-location visit.

Agricultural towns and foothill access

The Central Valley is more than the major freeway cities. Trucks and buses may be based at farms, packing operations, construction yards or smaller communities between the main corridors.

For Sonora and Tuolumne County, access and travel time matter. For agricultural routes, grouping multiple vehicles at one location usually produces the cleanest schedule.

Before we schedule

Have the city, vehicle count, engine years and deadline ready. For buses or vehicles parked at different locations, tell us the operating window so we can build the route correctly.
