Highway 101, not the I-880 unit

Novato, San Rafael, San Francisco, South San Francisco, San Mateo, Redwood City, Palo Alto, and the run down toward San Jose are this 101 route. Trucks sitting on a 101 yard should book here.

People who search “CARB test Bay Area” land on this 101 corridor instead of a generic East Bay list.

Engine model year picks the test: OBD $75 for 2013 and newer diesels (and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines), Mobile OVI $199 for 2012 and older diesels. We submit to CARB the same day. Due dates come from CARB’s Clean Truck Check portal.

What a 101 stop looks like

Bring the trucks to whatever still spot you already use — a yard in South San Francisco, a street-legal staging lot in San Francisco, a Peninsula warehouse, or a Novato fence line. We need room to complete an OBD scan or, on older diesels, an SAE J1667 snap-acceleration opacity test plus the visual inspection. We do not need a shop bay.

San Francisco work has its own friction: limited parking, load zones, and jobsites that cannot spare a driver for a bridge crossing. The Bayshore visit and the downtown I-Drill stop are in the field notes below. If your trucks live on the east side of the bay instead, send them to Hayward or the main Bay Area page.

On the north end, Novato and San Rafael are 101 cities. If the yard is actually in Petaluma, Santa Rosa, or up-valley in Napa or St. Helena, the better first read is Napa and Sonoma, and we will still come out. On the south end, San Jose yards that sit closer to I-880 can use Hayward or the main Bay page; 101-side San Jose still starts here.
