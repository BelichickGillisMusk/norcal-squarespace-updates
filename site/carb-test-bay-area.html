FAQ

Q: How much does a Clean Truck Check cost in Bay Area?
A: NorCal CARB Mobile charges HD-OBD $75 per truck for 2013+ diesel and 2018+ alt-fuel engines, Mobile OVI $199 per truck for pre-2013 and non-OBD engines, and $99 OBD / $229 OVI for motorhomes. That is the tester price for a mobile test at your yard. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: Do you come to my yard on the Highway 101 corridor?
A: Yes. NorCal CARB Mobile comes to your yard, shop, office, or jobsite in Bay Area for a mobile test. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: Which test does my truck need, OBD or OVI?
A: Engine model year decides the test, not the cab year. 2013 and newer diesel engines, and 2018 and newer alt-fuel engines, get the HD-OBD test for $75. Older and non-OBD engines get the Mobile OVI smoke opacity test for $199. Check the engine emissions label, then call (916) 890-4427.

Q: Do you test fleets in Bay Area?
A: Yes. NorCal CARB Mobile tests fleets at your yard, and one visit can cover OBD trucks and older OVI units. Fleet of 3 or more trucks? Call for fleet rates: (916) 890-4427.

Q: Is Bridget the Bridge Tester a real person?
A: No. Bridget the Bridge Tester is a cartoon mascot for Bay Area, not a real person or a member of our crew. NorCal CARB Mobile does the real Clean Truck Check at your yard. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.
