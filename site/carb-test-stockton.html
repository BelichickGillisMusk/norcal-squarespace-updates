Stockton calls here are port yards, warehouse docks, and ranch gates. Lathrop and Manteca are the distribution stops. Lodi is the ag haul. Tracy, Ripon, Escalon, French Camp, and Mountain House ride the same San Joaquin unit.

People search “CARB test Stockton” as often as they search “Clean Truck Check Stockton.” This route answers with the port and the three freeways. Industries, harvest, and fleet scheduling are on Stockton details.

What to have ready when you call

City, yard or dock name, truck count, and engine years if you can read them off the emission control label. If some vehicles are at the Port of Stockton and others are at a Lathrop warehouse the same morning, that is one San Joaquin visit — two pins.

East Bay I-880 yards and Highway 101 trucks are a different dispatch. Use the links above so each freeway goes to the right route.

Port rhythm versus ranch rhythm

A Port of Stockton or Lathrop dock often wants several tractors tested between shifts. A Lodi ranch gate often wants one or two trucks tested between loads. Both are this route. The equipment we bring depends on engine year, not on whether the pin says “port” or “vineyard.”

If the only vehicle you have is a diesel motorhome stored in Stockton, the motorhome card still applies ($99 OBD / $229 OVI) and the same phone books it.
