FAQ

Q: My trucks only run seasonally for harvest. Do they still need testing?
A: If they operate on California public roads over 14,000 lbs GVWR, yes — Clean Truck Check applies. Some agricultural vehicles follow CARB’s annual table. Your VIN’s date in CARB’s portal is the one that counts. Call (916) 890-4427.

Q: Can you test multiple trucks in one visit?
A: Yes. Fleet visits are our core business. For a fleet of 3 or more trucks, call (916) 890-4427 for fleet rates.

Q: Do you test at the Port of Stockton?
A: Yes. We test at the yard, dock, or staging lot. HD-OBD is $75 and Mobile OVI is $199. Call (916) 890-4427.

Q: Are Fairfield and Vacaville on this page?
A: No. Those cities are on the Fairfield and I-80 page. This page is Stockton, Lodi, Manteca, Tracy, Lathrop, and the rest of the San Joaquin corridor. Call (916) 890-4427.
