Manteca Clean Truck Check — a core stop, not a leftover

Manteca sits between Stockton freight and Modesto ag. Highway 99, Highway 120, and the 205 connector put mixers, dump trucks, landscape fleets, and small carriers in the same town. That is why this page treats Manteca as its own market.

Who we test in Manteca

  • Concrete, paving, and construction fleets that cannot sit in a station line before a pour
  • Dump, dirt, and demolition trucks based south of Stockton
  • Owner-operators parking at home or a rented yard off 120 / 99
  • Landscape, tree, and municipal contractors with 14,001+ lb diesels
  • Diesel motorhomes stored in Manteca neighborhoods and storage lots

How a Manteca visit works

Give us the address, engine years, and how many trucks will be on site. We come to the yard, run HD-OBD or OVI, and submit to CARB the same day. If some units are in Ripon or Lathrop that morning, say so — we can often string the south county loop in one trip.

Public prices on a Manteca stop: OBD $75 · OVI $199. If a truck fails, you get a plain-language readout so a diesel shop can fix it before retest.

Oakdale Clean Truck Check — east-side yards and 120 / 108 haul

Oakdale is the east gate of this market: dairy and feed toward the foothills, almonds and walnuts at harvest, and construction trucks that run 120 west to Manteca and 108 toward Sonora. We come to the dairy, the packing yard, or the house where the truck parks.

Who we test in Oakdale

  • Dairy, feed, and livestock haulers
  • Harvest and commodity trucks that only run part of the year
  • Construction and well-service trucks based east of Modesto
  • Riverbank / Oakdale owner-operators who do not want a Modesto station run
  • Diesel RVs stored in Oakdale and the Highway 108 corridor

Seasonal trucks still count

If the unit operates on California public roads and is over 14,000 lb GVWR, Clean Truck Check still applies — even if it only moves almonds, silage, or cattle for a few months. Waiting until harvest week is how registration holds happen.

Oakdale is on the same published card as Stockton and Manteca: OBD $75 · OVI $199 · motorhome $99 / $229. Group Oakdale with Escalon, Ripon, or Modesto when several trucks can sit in one morning.

Stockton / Manteca / Oakdale FAQ

What does a test cost in Stockton, Manteca, or Oakdale?

Published HD-OBD is $75 per vehicle. OVI / smoke is $199. Motorhomes are $99 OBD / $229 OVI. Same card on this core route.

Can you test in Manteca and Oakdale the same week?

Yes. Both are core stops on the Stockton loop. If you have trucks in both towns, we will try to stack them on one south-county or 120 day so you are not paying two trips in spirit — and we still do not add a trip fee on this route.

My Oakdale trucks only run harvest. Do they still need testing?

If they operate on California public roads over 14,000 lb GVWR, yes. Clean Truck Check is not waived because the season is short. Book before harvest so a hold does not hit when the almonds actually move.

Do you come to the Port of Stockton or a Manteca jobsite?

Yes. Yard, dock, shop, job staging lot, or driveway. We need safe access to the OBD port or exhaust and a few minutes per truck. Fleet visits are the core of this route.
