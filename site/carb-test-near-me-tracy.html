FAQ

Q: How much does a Clean Truck Check cost in Tracy?
A: NorCal CARB Mobile charges HD-OBD $75 per truck for 2013+ diesel and 2018+ alt-fuel engines, Mobile OVI $199 per truck for pre-2013 and non-OBD engines, and $99 OBD / $229 OVI for motorhomes. That is the tester price for a mobile test at your yard. CARB also charges a separate annual compliance fee, currently $32.13, rising to $33.12 for deadlines on and after January 1, 2027 — paid to CARB, not to us. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: How do I register for the Clean Truck Check program?
A: Register the vehicle in CARB's Clean Truck Check Vehicle Inspection System (CTC-VIS) at cleantruckcheck.arb.ca.gov. That registration is with CARB, not with NorCal CARB Mobile. Once the vehicle is in CTC-VIS, we come to your yard in Tracy for the mobile test. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: I have a DMV registration hold. What happens after the test?
A: NorCal CARB Mobile tests at your yard and submits the result to CARB. How quickly a DMV registration hold clears is up to CARB and DMV, and we do not promise a clock for that. Call (916) 890-4427 to book a mobile test.

Q: How often do I have to do a Clean Truck Check?
A: Most trucks need a test twice a year. California-registered motorhomes used for recreation or emergency occupancy, and qualifying agricultural vehicles, test once a year. CARB has said OBD-equipped trucks move to four tests a year starting October 1, 2027; one March 2024 CARB fact sheet instead says 2028. Check CARB's current schedule for your vehicle, or call NorCal CARB Mobile at (916) 890-4427.

Q: Do you come to my yard in Tracy?
