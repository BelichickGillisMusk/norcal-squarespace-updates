Hayward is an I-880 yard stop

Hayward calls are industrial yards on I-880, including Industrial Boulevard. A lot of these trucks run drayage toward the Port of Oakland or sit on East Bay construction work. We park at the yard and test there.

Union City is on the same corridor. A real route day has already included a Union City stop on the way back from a longer haul. Fremont, Newark, Castro Valley, San Leandro, San Lorenzo, Ashland, and Cherryland ride the same unit.

Mixed engine years are normal. One visit can cover OBD trucks and older OVI units. Engine model year decides the test, not the cab year.

OBD or OVI?

Engine model year, not cab year. 2013 and newer engines are the OBD test at $75. 2012 and older are the OVI smoke opacity test at $199.

Where are the official rules?

CARB publishes the program at cleantruckcheck.arb.ca.gov. We test and submit. We do not invent deadlines or fines.
