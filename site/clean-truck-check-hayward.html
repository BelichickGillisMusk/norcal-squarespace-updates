Hayward is an I-880 yard stop

Hayward calls are industrial yards on I-880, including the Industrial Boulevard corridor. A lot of these trucks run drayage toward the Port of Oakland or sit on East Bay construction work. We park at the yard and test there.

Union City is on the same corridor. A real route day has already included a Union City stop on the way back from a longer haul. Fremont, Newark, Castro Valley, San Leandro, San Lorenzo, Ashland, and Cherryland ride the same unit.

Mixed engine years are normal. One visit can cover OBD trucks and older Mobile OVI units. Engine model year on the emission control label decides the test, not the cab year.

This page is I-880. It is not the whole East Bay stuffed into one freeway name.

What is not on this page

Richmond is north East Bay on I-80 / I-580. Concord is inland East Bay on I-680 / Highway 4. Both are served from the same Oakland hub, and both belong on the Bay Area page and the East Bay directory — not on an I-880 city list.

Oakland itself is the hub city. Broader Oakland / Hayward / Richmond mornings also live on Oakland mobile CARB. Highway 101 from Novato through San Francisco and the Peninsula toward San Jose is CARB test across the Bay Area.

Industries along I-880

Drayage toward the Port of Oakland. Trucks staged in Hayward, San Leandro, or Fremont often run to the port and back the same day. Testing them at the yard beats burning a shift and a set of bridge tolls on a station trip.

Warehouse and distribution. I-880 is a warehouse spine. Fleet visits — several trucks, one appointment — are the usual booking.

Construction. Contractor dumps, boom trucks, and crew vehicles that live on this corridor get tested at the staging lot so the crew stays on the job.

Agricultural equipment that rolls on public roads. If it is a heavy-duty vehicle over 14,000 lbs GVWR using California public roads, Clean Truck Check applies. We will confirm the likely test before we dispatch.

Pricing

Same published NorCal rates as the rest of the Bay.

  • HD-OBD periodic test — $75 per vehicle (2013+ diesel and 2018+ alt-fuel).
  • Mobile OVI smoke opacity test — $199 per vehicle (2012 and older diesels: SAE J1667 plus visual inspection).
  • Motorhome and RV — $99 OBD / $229 OVI.

Fleets that use NorCal Family — Full Care still pay CARB’s annual portal fee plus $40 per year to us. That $40 is not a test price.

We submit every test to CARB the same day. Due dates live in CARB’s Clean Truck Check portal, not on this page. We do not invent fines.

Booking an I-880 window

Call (916) 890-4427 or send the yard and the truck count from the contact page. Tell us whether the vehicles will be at the same fence line or split between two lots (Hayward and Union City on the same morning is a normal conversation). Have engine years ready if you can read the emission control label — that is what picks OBD versus Mobile OVI, not the door badge.

From the Oakland hub, I-880 mornings are a straight run. We do not invent a due date while we are on the phone. If you are inside the 90-day window before a Clean Truck Check deadline, say so and we will put you on the next open slot. If a truck fails and needs repairs, schedule the follow-up with us.

For the longer background on why East Bay fleets test at the yard instead of deadheading to a station, read Clean Truck Check in Hayward, Union City, Fremont, and Newark. The official program rules sit at CARB’s Clean Truck Check testing page.

A Hayward yard is also a common staging point for trucks that later sit in Oakland or San Leandro. If the whole fleet moves during the week, pick the morning they will be parked longest and we will meet them there.

FAQ

Q: Which cities are on this Hayward page?
A: Hayward, Union City, Fremont, Newark, Castro Valley, San Leandro, San Lorenzo, Ashland, and Cherryland — the I-880 industrial corridor. Richmond and Concord are East Bay too, but they are not I-880 cities. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: OBD or OVI?
A: Engine model year, not cab year. 2013 and newer engines are the OBD test at $75. 2012 and older are the OVI smoke opacity test at $199.

Q: Where are the official rules?
A: CARB publishes the program at cleantruckcheck.arb.ca.gov. We test and submit. We do not invent deadlines or fines.
