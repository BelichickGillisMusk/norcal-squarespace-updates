Clean Truck Check

Clean Truck Check (California’s heavy-duty inspection and maintenance program) is about keeping high-emitting trucks off the road and keeping your registration clear. Newer engines usually take an OBD readout. Older equipment may need an opacity / OVI path. We test what the program requires for the vehicle in front of us and submit results through the proper channels so you are not guessing about paperwork.
