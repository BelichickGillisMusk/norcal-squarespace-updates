Clean Truck Check facts E01–E08

Each line is the closed-caption fact for that Short. Info from CARB Clean Truck Check. This page is not a CARB endorsement.

  1. E01 — Who needs a Clean Truck Check?

    Vehicles over 14,000 lb GVWR on CA roads need Clean Truck Check.

    Clean Truck Check applies to most diesel and alternative-fuel heavy-duty vehicles over 14,000 pounds GVWR that operate on California roads.

  2. E02 — Three steps owners can’t skip

    Report in CTC-VIS, pay the annual fee, then get tested.

    Vehicle owners report in the Clean Truck Check Vehicle Information System (CTC-VIS), pay the annual fee, and get a compliance test from a credentialed tester.

  3. E03 — Can anyone submit your test?

    Only credentialed testers can submit Clean Truck Check results.

    Only CARB-credentialed testers can submit Clean Truck Check results. Credentials expire every two years.

  4. E04 — When can you test?

    You can test up to 90 days before your deadline.

    You can complete testing up to 90 days before your compliance deadline. Passing results can be submitted in that window.

  5. E05 — What if you skip it?

    Skipping can mean DMV holds and notices to test.

    Enforcement can include DMV registration holds, facility entry denials, and notices to test.

  6. E06 — OBD or smoke test?

    OBD trucks plug in; older trucks need a smoke opacity test.

    Newer OBD-equipped trucks use an OBD Clean Truck Check. Older or non-OBD vehicles need a smoke opacity (OVI) test. OBD is $75. OVI is $199. See Clean Truck Check services and pricing.

  7. E07 — Do motorhomes count?

    CA-registered motorhomes over 14,000 lb are covered.

    California-registered motorhomes over 14,000 pounds GVWR are covered by Clean Truck Check.

  8. E08 — Why go mobile?

    We test at your yard and submit results for you.

    A mobile credentialed tester can run your Clean Truck Check at your yard and submit results for you. Call 916-890-4427.
