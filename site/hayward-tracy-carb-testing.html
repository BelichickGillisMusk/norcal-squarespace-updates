Give us the address, engine years, and how many trucks will be on site. We come to you, run HD-OBD or OVI, and submit to CARB the same day. If some units are in Livermore or Tracy that afternoon, say so — that is the point of this corridor page.

Hayward to Tracy FAQ

What does a test cost on this corridor?

Published HD-OBD is $75 per vehicle. OVI / smoke is $199. Motorhomes are $99 OBD / $229 OVI. No mileage add-on on this core route.

Can you test Hayward and Tracy the same day?

Often yes, when both yards can have trucks ready. Tell us both addresses and engine years on the call so we build 580 in one direction instead of two trips.

Do you test at distribution docks in Tracy or Livermore?

Yes. Yard, dock, shop, job staging lot, or driveway. We need safe access to the OBD port or exhaust and a few minutes per truck. Fleet visits are the core of this route.

Do you test diesel motorhomes in Pleasanton or Livermore?

Yes. Class A, B, and C diesel coaches at the storage lot or driveway. We confirm OBD vs OVI from the engine year before we roll.

Is this the same company as the Hayward sister site?

Scheduling, pricing, and invoicing are NorCal CARB Mobile at 916-890-4427. Do not use other phone numbers from test landing pages.
