Communities on this route

Napa · St. Helena · Calistoga · Yountville · American Canyon · Santa Rosa · Sonoma

American Canyon also appears on the Fairfield / I-80 page because SR-29 connects those yards to the I-80 spine. Livermore does not belong here. Livermore is Tri-Valley / I-580 and books on the Bay Area page.

Santa Rosa is Sonoma County. It is not part of a “Butte over to Santa Rosa” region. Butte County has its own page.

Built for working wine-country fleets

Vehicles in Napa and Sonoma are not always parked beside the highway. Wineries, agricultural operators, contractors, and hospitality fleets often need testing arranged around gates, deliveries, crush, and working hours. Tell us where the vehicles are kept and when they are available; we will build the visit around the operation.

Industries on this route:

  • Wineries and hospitality — delivery trucks, utility diesels, and support vehicles that cannot block a gate during tasting or crush.
  • Agriculture — orchard, vineyard, and ranch trucks that use public roads. Some qualify for CARB’s annual agricultural table; the portal date for that VIN wins.
  • Contractors — construction, excavation, and landscape fleets parked at yards between Napa, Sonoma, and Santa Rosa.
  • Corridor overflow — trucks that live closer to Vallejo or Fairfield should start on the I-80 page.

Petaluma sits on the Sonoma edge of this conversation and on Highway 101. A published field note already names a Petaluma construction stop as part of a multi-county day. See the related field note.

How a wine-country visit is scheduled

Call (916) 890-4427 with the town, the gate or yard, the truck count, and the hours someone can meet us. Crush, delivery days, and tasting-room hours are useful to know up front so we are not parked across a drive when a truck needs to leave.

Highway 29 and Highway 12 are the usual spines; Santa Rosa can also be reached off 101. If the vehicles actually sit in Vallejo, Fairfield, or Vacaville, start on the I-80 page. If they sit in Novato or San Rafael, use the Highway 101 door.

We submit to CARB the same day. We do not invent a due date while standing at the gate.

What stays official

Engine year on the emission control label, not the door badge, picks the test. HD-OBD at $75. Mobile OVI at $199. Recreational CA-registered motorhomes are generally annual; commercial-use motorhomes follow the twice-a-year table. We submit to CARB. CARB issues compliance. No windshield sticker from us, and no invented fine print at the gate.
