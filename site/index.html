CARB 5-day pass (official link)

Most drivers never find this. It’s a CARB temporary pass — not a substitute for a Clean Truck Check. We put the real link here so you’re not hunting PDFs.

  • Once per VIN per calendar year · five consecutive calendar days
  • Request through CTC-VIS (CARB’s portal) — keep approval in the truck
  • Does not make the truck fully “compliant” long-term · still need your real OBD/OVI when due
