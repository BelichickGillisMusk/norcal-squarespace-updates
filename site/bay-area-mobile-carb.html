Warehouse, cross-dock, and food distribution. I-880 and the Peninsula are full of warehouse rows and cross-docks. A fleet visit is the normal product: one appointment, every truck on the list, mixed OBD and older OVI units in the same stop when the engine years call for it.

Diesel motorhomes and RVs. Class A, B, and C diesels over 14,000 lbs GVWR that use California public roads are in Clean Truck Check. What the test is

Clean Truck Check (heavy-duty inspection and maintenance) applies to most non-gasoline heavy-duty vehicles over 14,000 lbs GVWR on California public roads, including out-of-state trucks that operate here. Zero-emission vehicles, military tactical vehicles, emergency vehicles, historical plates, out-of-state recreational motorhomes, and gasoline vehicles are out.

The test follows engine year on the emission control label, not the cab or chassis year.

  • HD-OBD, $75 per vehicle. A scan of the engine OBD data on a CARB-certified device. Diesel engines model year 2013 and newer, and alternative-fuel engines model year 2018 and newer.
  • Mobile OVI, $199 per vehicle. Shop language for opacity and visual inspection. CARB does not call it “OVI.” For diesel engines model year 2012 and older it is an SAE J1667 smoke opacity snap-acceleration test plus a visual inspection of emissions-control equipment. Alternative-fuel engines 2017 and older are visual only, no opacity. An off-road engine in an on-road heavy-duty vehicle takes opacity plus visual, any year. We never call this an “eye test.”
  • Diesel motorhome / RV: $99 OBD / $229 OVI.

For a fleet of 3 or more trucks, call for fleet rates: (916) 890-4427.

Almost all subject vehicles test twice a year. California-registered deadlines follow the DMV registration expiration month and six months later. Agricultural vehicles (CARB’s definition) and CA-registered motorhomes used for recreation or emergency occupancy test once a year. Tests may be submitted up to 90 days before a compliance deadline. Beginning October 2027, OBD-equipped vehicles move to four tests a year; non-commercial motorhomes and agricultural vehicles stay annual. Your VIN’s date in CARB’s Clean Truck Check portal wins over anything written on a website.

We perform the test and submit it. CARB issues compliance. There is no windshield sticker from us, and a passing test is not a promise that every other CARB or DMV issue is cleared. Deadlines and fines come from CARB.
