Bay Area FAQ

Q: Who does mobile Clean Truck Check in the Bay Area?
A: NorCal CARB Mobile. We come to your yard for mobile HD-OBD and OVI smoke opacity testing in Oakland, Hayward, Fremont, and the East Bay. HD-OBD is $75 per truck and OVI is $199, with $0 travel in zone. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: Is there a Clean Truck Check near me in the Bay Area?
A: Yes. We test at your yard, office, or jobsite in Oakland, Hayward, Fremont, San Leandro, Union City, and along the East Bay I-880 corridor. Weekends are available. Call (916) 890-4427 to set a time.

Q: How much does a Clean Truck Check cost?
A: Our mobile test prices are HD-OBD $75 per truck, OVI $199, and motorhome testing $99 OBD / $229 OVI. Fleet of 3 or more trucks? Call for fleet rates: (916) 890-4427. CARB also charges a separate annual compliance fee in CTC-VIS; that fee is $33.12 for vehicle compliance deadlines on and after January 1, 2027.

Q: Which test do I need, OBD or OVI?
A: HD-OBD is $75 for 2013 and newer diesel engines and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines. Mobile OVI is $199 for pre-2013 diesel and other non-OBD engines. The test follows the engine year on the emission control label. Call (916) 890-4427 if you want help choosing before we come out.

Q: When is my Clean Truck Check due?
A: Most subject vehicles test on a semi-annual schedule. A credentialed tester may submit a passing test up to 90 days before the compliance deadline. Beginning October 2027, OBD-equipped vehicles will be required to undergo testing four times per year. Confirm your dates in CTC-VIS.

Q: How long does a DMV registration hold take to clear after I complete a Clean Truck Check?
A: CARB says the SB 210 registration hold is removed 1 to 3 business days after the compliance requirements for the upcoming deadline have been completed, including the annual compliance fee and a passing emissions test. If a registration hold is already in place, CARB's FAQ says to allow 3 to 5 business days from the date the compliance certificate is downloaded for DMV to update. Weekends and holidays do not count as business days. After the hold is removed, you still need to contact DMV to finish the registration. We submit the test the same day; the hold itself follows CARB's timeframe.

Q: Is travel really $0?
A: In our East Bay / Bay Area zone (Oakland, Hayward, Fremont, San Leandro, Richmond, Concord, Alameda & Contra Costa), travel is $0. Call (916) 890-4427 if you are unsure.
