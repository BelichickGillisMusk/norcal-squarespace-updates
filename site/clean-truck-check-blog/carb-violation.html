Got a CARB Clean Truck Check Letter in Sacramento? 3 Steps to Take Today

Published January 18, 2026 · Updated November 19, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile

Got a CARB Clean Truck Check letter at your Sacramento yard? Log in to CTC-VIS to see what's actually missing (reporting, the annual fee or a passing test), then book the right test so a passing result reaches CARB before the letter's deadline.

Call (916) 890-4427 to book. OBD is $75 per truck, OVI is $199, and motorhomes are $99/$229. Fleet of 3 or more trucks? Call for fleet rates: (916) 890-4427.

Step 1: Check CTC-VIS before you do anything else

Start with a letter-in-hand portal check: open the CARB letter, log into CTC-VIS, and confirm what is actually due before you book the test.

CTC-VIS is CARB's Clean Truck Check reporting system. A truck is compliant when it is reported there, its annual fee is paid, it has a passing test for the current deadline, and it has no open enforcement violations. The letter tells you what kind of notice it is. CTC-VIS tells you what's missing.

If it's a Notice to Submit to Testing: CARB wants a passing test within 30 calendar days. If you miss that deadline, CARB says you could get a Notice of Violation and a penalty.

Step 2: Pay the annual fee if it's open

The fee is paid in CTC-VIS. It's $32.13 per vehicle now, and $33.12 for deadlines on or after January 1, 2027. CTC-VIS shows the exact amount due for each truck. Card payments take 1 to 2 business days to process. eChecks can take up to 7.

Step 3: Book the right test

It goes by the engine model year on the emission control label, not the year on the door.

  • OBD test ($75 per truck): 2013 and newer diesel engines, and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines.
  • OVI ($199): 2012 and older diesel engines get a smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions equipment.
  • Motorhome ($99/$229): Call and we'll tell you which one fits.

A non-compliant truck isn't supposed to run on California roads until it's compliant, so the test should come to the truck. We come to your yard, so you don't burn $8 diesel. The credentialed tester sends the passing result to CARB. You don't upload it.

If there's a DMV registration hold

CARB says DMV places registration holds automatically on trucks that aren't compliant. Once everything due is done, CARB says the hold comes off 1 to 3 business days later. Weekends and holidays don't count. DMV won't mail your tags on its own, so you still need to contact DMV to finish the registration.

Questions and answers

How long do I have after a Notice to Submit to Testing? 30 calendar days to submit a passing test, per CARB.

Is paying the fee enough? No. The fee and the test are separate. Most trucks need both by each deadline.

How fast does a DMV hold clear? CARB says 1 to 3 business days after everything due is done. Then contact DMV to finish the registration.

Can you test the truck where it's parked? Yes. We test at your yard or jobsite around Sacramento. Call (916) 890-4427.
