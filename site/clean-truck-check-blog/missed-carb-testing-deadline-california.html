Missed a CARB Testing Deadline in California?

Published September 13, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile Team

A missed Clean Truck Check deadline is a paperwork and test problem, not a reason to invent a new route. Senate Bill 210 (SB 210 / Q212) lets DMV place a registration hold when a subject vehicle is missing registration in the program, the annual fee, or a passing test. Civil penalties can compound while that stay open. Confirm status in the CTC-VIS portal at cleantruckcheck.arb.ca.gov.

What the hold actually is

The 2026 Clean Truck Check compliance fee is $32.13 per vehicle per year, paid in CTC-VIS. That is the CARB fee, not our test price. Test prices are on pricing.

Ports and railyards may check compliance status at the gate. A bay bridge pickle ticket on the dash does not clear SB 210. The portal record does.

If you received an NST

A Notice to Submit to Testing (NST) is a separate clock. It gives 30 days to submit a passing Clean Truck Check. That window is not the same as the regular deadline month.

The path back

  1. Register the VIN in CTC-VIS if it is not already there.
  2. Pay the $32.13 fee.
  3. Complete a passing test.
  4. Confirm the result uploaded in Testing Details.

After those pieces are in, the hold often clears in 1–3 business days.
