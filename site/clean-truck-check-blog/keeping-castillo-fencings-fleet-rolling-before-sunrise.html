OVI or OBD: which one your trucks need

It goes by the engine model year on the emission control label, not the year on the door.

  • OVI ($199): 2012 and older diesel engines. That's the SAE J1667 smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions equipment.
  • OBD test ($75 per truck): 2013 and newer diesel engines, and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines.
  • Motorhome ($99/$229): Call and we'll tell you which one fits.

The tester sends the passing result to CARB. You don't upload anything.

What else each truck needs

1. Report it in CTC-VIS, CARB's Clean Truck Check reporting system. 2. Pay the annual compliance fee: $32.13 per vehicle now, $33.12 for deadlines on or after January 1, 2027. 3. Pass the test by each deadline. Most trucks test twice a year, every six months. Starting October 2027, OBD-equipped trucks move to four times a year.

You can submit a passing test up to 90 days before a deadline. Testing early leaves time to fix a problem before the date.
