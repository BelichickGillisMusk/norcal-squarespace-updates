Your Guide to Clean Truck Check Deadlines and Requirements for 2026: Sacramento and NorCal

Published August 7, 2025 · Updated November 19, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile

For Sacramento-area trucks registered in California, Clean Truck Check deadlines follow your DMV registration expiration month and come around again six months later. Your CTC-VIS account shows the official dates, and you can test up to 90 days before each one.

What you need at each deadline

To run a heavy-duty truck on California roads, it has to be compliant with the Clean Truck Check (CARB's Heavy-Duty Inspection and Maintenance program). That means:

1. Reported in CTC-VIS, CARB's Clean Truck Check reporting system. 2. Annual fee paid, due at the first deadline of each compliance year. It's $32.13 per vehicle now, and $33.12 for deadlines on or after January 1, 2027. 3. No open enforcement violations. 4. A passing emissions test from a CARB credentialed tester.

Miss a deadline and CARB says you can face DMV registration blocks and other enforcement.

Who it covers

Nearly all diesel and alternative-fuel trucks, buses and other vehicles over 14,000 lbs gross vehicle weight rating that operate in California. That includes government vehicles, private vehicles, hybrids, and trucks registered in other states.

Finding your 2026 dates

California-registered trucks: your DMV registration expiration month sets the schedule. We can do a sticker-month deadline walk with you: your DMV registration month sets the first date, and the second date falls six months later. A March registration means March and September. A November registration means May and November.

Trucks registered outside California, and DMV-exempt plates (government fleets): the last digit of the VIN sets the schedule, also every six months. A truck that was ever registered in California stays on the DMV-month schedule.

Non-commercial motorhomes and agricultural vehicles: once a year, in the DMV registration month.

Either way, the dates in CTC-VIS are the ones that count.

What changes in October 2027

Starting October 2027, OBD-equipped trucks move to four tests a year. Non-commercial motorhomes and agricultural vehicles stay on once a year.

Which test your truck needs

It goes by the engine model year on the emission control label, not the year on the door.

  • OBD test ($75 per truck): 2013 and newer diesel engines, and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines.
  • OVI ($199): 2012 and older diesel engines get a smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions equipment.
  • 2017 and older alternative-fuel engines get the visual inspection only.
  • Motorhome ($99/$229): Call and we'll tell you which one fits.

Use the 90-day window

A passing test can be submitted up to 90 days before a deadline. CARB's example: a truck due February 1 can submit as early as November 3. That leaves time to fix a problem and retest. CTC-VIS also emails a monthly summary of trucks coming due in the next 90 days.

Questions and answers

When is my Clean Truck Check due in 2026? For a California-registered truck, in your DMV registration month and six months later. CTC-VIS has the official dates.

Can I test early? Yes, up to 90 days before the deadline.

Is it four times a year now? No. Four times a year starts in October 2027 for OBD-equipped trucks.
