Is Clean Truck Check Testing Four Times a Year? The 2026 Answer for Sacramento Fleets

Published February 19, 2026 · Updated November 19, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile

Not in 2026: Sacramento-area trucks are still on two Clean Truck Check deadlines a year, set by the DMV registration month and the month six months later. Four tests a year starts in October 2027 for OBD-equipped trucks, while non-commercial motorhomes and agricultural vehicles stay on once a year.

What's true right now

In 2026 most trucks still run on a double-date registration clock: one deadline in the DMV month and one six months later, not four times a year. Trucks registered outside California and DMV-exempt plates go by the last digit of the VIN instead, also every six months.

What changes in October 2027

Beginning October 2027, OBD-equipped trucks move to four tests a year. Non-commercial motorhomes and agricultural vehicles, as CARB's regulation defines them, stay on a once-a-year schedule. Your CTC-VIS account will show the dates for each truck, so check there rather than counting months yourself.

Which trucks are OBD-equipped

It goes by the engine model year on the emission control label, not the year on the door.

  • OBD test ($75 per truck): 2013 and newer diesel engines, and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines. These are the trucks that move to four tests a year in October 2027.
  • OVI ($199): 2012 and older diesel engines get a smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions equipment.
  • Motorhome ($99/$229): Call and we'll tell you which one fits.

Where to find your dates

Your deadlines are in your CTC-VIS account, CARB's Clean Truck Check reporting system. CARB also emails a monthly summary listing the trucks with a deadline in the next 90 days and any open enforcement actions. A passing test can be submitted up to 90 days before a deadline.

More tests means the trip matters more

Twice a year, a station run is a hassle. Four times a year, it adds up fast. We come to your yard, so you don't burn $8 diesel. The tester sends each passing result to CARB, and you don't upload anything.

Each truck also owes the annual fee in CTC-VIS: $32.13 per vehicle now, and $33.12 for deadlines on or after January 1, 2027.
