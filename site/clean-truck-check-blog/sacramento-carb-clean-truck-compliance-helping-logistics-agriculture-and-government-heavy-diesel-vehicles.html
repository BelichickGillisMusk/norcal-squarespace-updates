What the Clean Truck Check asks of you in 2026

The Clean Truck Check (CARB’s Heavy-Duty Inspection and Maintenance program) covers almost all non-gasoline heavy-duty vehicles over 14,000 lbs gross vehicle weight rating that operate in California. Trucks registered outside California count too when they run here. Here’s what each truck needs:

  1. Report the truck in CTC-VIS, CARB’s Clean Truck Check reporting system.
  2. Pay the annual compliance fee. It’s currently $32.13 per vehicle. For compliance deadlines on or after January 1, 2027, it goes up to $33.12. CARB adjusts it every year with the California Consumer Price Index.
  3. Pass an emissions test done by a CARB credentialed tester, then make sure the truck is cleared by its deadline.

Miss a deadline and CARB says you can face DMV registration blocks and other enforcement.

How often you test in 2026 and 2027

  • 2026: Trucks on the semi-annual schedule have to show compliance every six months. Check your CTC-VIS account for your exact due dates, or see when your Clean Truck Check is due.
  • Starting October 2027: OBD-equipped trucks move to testing four times a year.
  • Non-commercial motorhomes and agricultural vehicles stay on a once-a-year schedule.

The 90-day window

A passing test can be submitted up to 90 days before your compliance deadline. CARB’s own example: a truck due February 1 can submit a passing test as early as November 3. Testing early gives you room to fix anything before the deadline.

Which test does your truck need?

It depends on the engine model year on the emission control label (ECL), not the year on the door.

  • OBD test ($75 per truck): 2013 and newer diesel engines, plus 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines. We scan the engine’s OBD data with a CARB certified OBD device.
  • OVI ($199): 2012 and older diesel engines. That’s a smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions control equipment. Read more about the OVI smoke opacity test in Sacramento.
  • Motorhome ($99/$229): Call and we’ll tell you which one fits your rig.

Your credentialed tester sends the passing result to CARB. You don’t upload it yourself.
