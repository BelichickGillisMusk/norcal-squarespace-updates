Who needs a Clean Truck Check in Stockton

The Clean Truck Check is CARB's Heavy-Duty Inspection and Maintenance program. It covers nearly all diesel and alternative-fuel vehicles over 14,000 lbs gross vehicle weight rating that operate on California roads. In and around Stockton, that means warehouse and cross-dock trucks, ag haulers, construction rigs, buses and diesel motorhomes.

Trucks plated in another state count too. CARB says vehicles registered outside California still have to comply when they run here, and CARB staff run roadside inspections at border crossings and outside ports and railyards.

Which test your truck needs

It goes by the engine model year on the emission control label, not the year on the door.

  • OBD test ($75 per truck): 2013 and newer diesel engines, plus 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines. We scan the engine's OBD data.
  • OVI ($199): 2012 and older diesel engines get a smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions equipment.
  • Motorhome ($99/$229): Call and we'll tell you which one fits.

How often you test

  • Now: most trucks show compliance twice a year, every six months.
  • Non-commercial motorhomes and agricultural vehicles: once a year.
  • Starting October 2027: OBD-equipped trucks move to four times a year.

Your exact dates are in your CTC-VIS account, CARB's Clean Truck Check reporting system. A passing test can be submitted up to 90 days before a deadline, so you have time to fix anything and retest.

What test day looks like at a Stockton yard

You don't pull trucks off the route or line up at a station. We set up where the trucks are parked, run each test, and the tester sends the result to CARB. You don't upload anything yourself. Treat a multi-truck Stockton visit as a Charter Way yard-lane sweep: we work down the row, test each truck, and submit to CARB.

We come to your yard, so you don't burn $8 diesel.

The annual fee

Each truck also owes CARB's annual compliance fee, paid in CTC-VIS. It's $32.13 per vehicle now, and $33.12 for deadlines on or after January 1, 2027. The fee is separate from the test.
