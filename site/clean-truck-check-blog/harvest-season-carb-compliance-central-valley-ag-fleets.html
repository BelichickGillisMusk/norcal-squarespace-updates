Harvest-Season CARB Compliance for Central Valley Ag Fleets

Published September 13, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile Team

Harvest does not pause a Clean Truck Check deadline. The deadline follows registration, not the crop calendar. Qualifying agricultural vehicles stay on an annual test — they are not exempt. We come to the yard across Central Valley counties: Fresno, Kings, Tulare, Madera, Merced, San Joaquin, Stanislaus, Sacramento, and Yolo.

What qualifies as an agricultural vehicle

An agricultural vehicle needs all three:

  • Owned or operated by a farming business
  • Used exclusively in agricultural operations
  • Transports agricultural products to the first point of processing

First point of processing means the first stop that alters, packs, or prepares the crop: a packinghouse, slaughterhouse, cotton gin, nut huller/sheller, dehydrator, grain/feed mill, lumber mill, or biomass facility.

Secondary runs do not qualify. A huller then a distribution center, a wholesale or retail drop, or an auction is past the first point of processing. The designation is per vehicle, and use must stay exclusive.

Annual test — not a harvest exemption

Qualifying ag vehicles take an annual test, not a semi-annual one. The Clean Truck Check compliance fee is the same $32.13 per vehicle per year, paid in CTC-VIS. Passing results may be submitted in the 90-day early window. The test itself is opacity or OBD as applicable for that engine.

Opacity in this post: 1991 and newer maximum 40%; pre-1991 maximum 55%.

Beginning October 1, 2027, OBD vehicles go quarterly. That change does not affect the 2026 harvest.

Official cadence: CARB emissions compliance testing requirements. Agricultural designation notes: CARB agricultural vehicles and California motorhomes annual requirements.

Harvest does not move the date

A tuesday pickle ticket on the harvest clipboard does not move CTC-VIS. Test 90 to 60 days early so a fail can be repaired before the hold. Seasonal use is not an exemption.

What is in scope — and what is not

Implements of husbandry that never use public roads are out of scope. On-road support trucks over 14,000 lb GVWR that are not gasoline are in scope.

Register, attest, and pay in CTC-VIS

Register the VIN in the CTC-VIS portal. Attest the agricultural designation: Step 5 checkbox, Step 6 attestation form, Step 7 sign. Then pay the fee.

SB 210 allows a DMV hold if registration, the fee, or a passing test is missing.
