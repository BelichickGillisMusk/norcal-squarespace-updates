Clean Truck Check for California Owner-Operators

Published September 13, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile Team

One truck is still in scope. Fleet size is not the test. If the unit is subject to Clean Truck Check, register it in the CTC-VIS portal, pay the annual fee, and keep a passing result on file.

What still applies to a single truck

  • The 2026 compliance fee is $32.13 per vehicle per year, paid in CTC-VIS.
  • Most owner-operator trucks are on a semi-annual test — two tests a year, not one.
  • Battery-electric (BEV) and fuel-cell (FCEV) vehicles are exempt from emissions testing.
  • Motorhomes and qualifying agricultural vehicles stay annual, not semi-annual, when they meet those definitions.
  • Update CTC-VIS within 30 days of a buy or sell.

Official cadence: CARB emissions compliance testing requirements.

We come to you

A visit is typically 20–45 minutes once the truck is ready. We come to you. First OBD test is $75; a second truck on the same visit is $37.50. Other test types stay on pricing.
