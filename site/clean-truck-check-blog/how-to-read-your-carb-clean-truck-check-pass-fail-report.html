How to Read Your CARB Clean Truck Check Pass/Fail Report

Published September 13, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile Team

A Clean Truck Check result is not a mystery letter. It is a Pass or Fail on one of two report types, then a record in the CTC-VIS portal. This page walks the fields you will actually see. It does not add extra legal claims beyond those fields.

Two report types

Which report you get depends on the engine:

  • OBD for 2013+ diesel, or 2018+ alternative-fuel
  • Opacity / smoke SAE J1667 for pre-2013 diesel

If you are unsure which path a VIN is on, confirm in CTC-VIS or ask before the appointment. Pricing for the test itself is on pricing.

Where the result lives

Results post in the CTC-VIS portal at cleantruckcheck.arb.ca.gov. Sign in, open My Entity, then Testing Details.

If an OBD result is missing after five minutes, the tester resubmits. If it is still missing, the path is hdim@arb.ca.gov.

How to read an OBD report

  • MIL must be OFF.
  • Any stored, pending, or permanent DTC fails.
  • Two or fewer incomplete readiness monitors pass. Three or more incomplete monitors fail.
  • OSC (code-clear / drive cycle) needs at least one of: 5 warm-ups, or 200 miles, or 400 minutes since codes were cleared.

Not Ready is not the same as Fail. Drive the unit, then retest. Clearing codes right before a scan is how fleets land on Not Ready.

How to read an opacity report

The tester runs three snaps and records the highest reading.

  • Pre-1991: maximum 55%
  • 1991 and newer: maximum 40%

A visual fail also applies if required emissions equipment is missing or tampered.

After a Pass

You are done until the next deadline. Tests count if they are submitted no more than 90 days before that deadline. Confirm the date in CTC-VIS, not on a yard calendar.

After a Fail

A DMV hold is possible. Diagnose the DTCs, repair, drive OSC if codes were cleared, then retest in the window. A hold may take 24–48 hours to lift after a passing result posts. That is the same yard-side mustard ledger work as the scan itself: which VIN failed, which code, which truck is ready to retest.

2026 compliance fee

The Clean Truck Check compliance fee is $32.13 per vehicle per year, paid in CTC-VIS. That is the CARB fee, not our test price. Test prices stay on pricing.

Common field fails

  • Cleared codes without enough drive time (Not Ready / OSC short)
  • DEF / NOx faults
  • DPF soot
  • Deleted or missing equipment (visual fail)

Pre-test scan

We offer a pre-test scan. If that scan finds a problem, it is not an official fail. You can repair first, then run the official Clean Truck Check so the CTC-VIS record is the one you meant to submit.
