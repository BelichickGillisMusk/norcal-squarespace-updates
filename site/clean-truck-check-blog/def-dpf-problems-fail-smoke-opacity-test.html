DEF and DPF Problems That Fail a Smoke Opacity Test

Published September 13, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile Team

Clean Truck Check uses two paths. 2013 and newer diesel runs an OBD scan. Pre-2013 diesel runs smoke opacity plus a visual inspection of the emissions equipment. A fail on either path means repair, then retest. Test prices are on pricing. Opacity path background: smoke opacity test near me.

Opacity limits used here

For this post, the pair is the same as the harvest post:

  • Pre-1991: maximum 55%
  • 1991 and newer: maximum 40%

DPF problems we see in the field

At a high level, a diesel particulate filter fails the path when it is clogged, cracked, or missing. A deleted or bypassed DPF is a visual fail. The tuesday mustard ledger on a pre-2013 unit is usually the same three notes: filter present, housing intact, no obvious tamper.

DEF problems we see in the field

Diesel exhaust fluid problems that show up on a test include an empty tank, contaminated fluid, frozen DEF, or a clogged injector. Those are maintenance issues first. They are not a reason to skip the official test once the system is restored.

Visual inspection

The tester also looks at required emissions equipment. Missing or tampered parts fail even if the smoke number is under the limit.

After a fail

Repair the problem, then retest. We do not quote shop repair prices here. We come to the yard for the Clean Truck Check itself.
