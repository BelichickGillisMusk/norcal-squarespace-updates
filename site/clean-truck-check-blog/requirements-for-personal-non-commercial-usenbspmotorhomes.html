Clean Truck Check for Personal-Use Motorhomes in Roseville and Sacramento

Published September 15, 2025 · Updated November 19, 2026 · NorCal CARB Mobile

Own a diesel motorhome over 14,000 lbs GVWR in Roseville or Sacramento? Even for personal use, it has to be reported in CTC-VIS, have its annual fee paid and pass a Clean Truck Check once a year, and we can test it in your driveway for $99 (OBD) or $229 (OVI).

Call (916) 890-4427 to book. OBD is $75 per truck, OVI is $199, and motorhomes are $99/$229. Fleet of 3 or more trucks? Call for fleet rates: (916) 890-4427.

Does your motorhome need it?

The Clean Truck Check (CARB's Heavy-Duty Inspection and Maintenance program) applies to your motorhome if it is:

  • Over 14,000 lbs manufacturer's gross vehicle weight rating. The original GVWR label is usually on the door jamb or inside the door.
  • Non-gasoline, which usually means diesel.
  • Registered in California. CARB's FAQ says motorhomes registered outside California aren't subject.

Once a year for personal use

California-registered motorhomes used for recreation or emergency occupancy test once a year. The deadline lands in your DMV registration expiration month, and CTC-VIS shows the exact date. Motorhomes used commercially follow the twice-a-year schedule instead.

The three things CARB wants

1. Report it in CTC-VIS, CARB's Clean Truck Check reporting system, with owner and vehicle information. 2. Pay the annual compliance fee in CTC-VIS by the deadline. It's $32.13 per vehicle now, and $33.12 for deadlines on or after January 1, 2027. CARB takes debit, credit or eCheck, not paper checks. 3. Pass an emissions test from a CARB credentialed tester. You can submit it up to 90 days before the deadline.

If a motorhome isn't compliant, CARB says DMV can place a registration hold on it.

Which test your coach needs

It goes by the engine model year on the emission control label, not the year of the coach.

  • Motorhome OBD ($99): 2013 and newer diesel engines, and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines.
  • Motorhome OVI ($229): 2012 and older diesel engines get a smoke opacity test plus a visual inspection of the emissions equipment.
  • 2017 and older alternative-fuel engines get the visual inspection only.

Testing in your driveway

You don't have to drive a 30-foot coach across town for a test. If the coach is parked at home, we can run a Roseville driveway coach check and submit the Clean Truck Check result to CARB. The tester sends the result to CARB, so there's nothing for you to upload.

How often? Once a year for personal use, in your DMV registration month.

What does it cost? $99 for a motorhome OBD test or $229 for a motorhome OVI.
