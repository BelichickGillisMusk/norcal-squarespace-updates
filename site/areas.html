What every NorCal visit includes

We come to the yard, office, or jobsite. Engine year on the emission control label picks the test: HD-OBD at $75 for 2013 and newer diesel engines and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines; Mobile OVI at $199 for 2012 and older diesels (SAE J1667 smoke opacity plus a visual inspection). We submit the result to CARB the same day. CARB issues compliance. Deadlines and fines come from CARB, and a DMV hold does not clear on a clock we can promise.

For a fleet of 3 or more trucks, call for fleet rates: (916) 890-4427.

FAQ

Q: Where do you test?
A: At your yard, office, or jobsite across Northern California — Sacramento, the Bay Area from our Oakland hub, Stockton, and scheduled routes into wine country, Butte County, the foothills, and the wider Central Valley. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: How much does a Clean Truck Check cost?
A: HD-OBD is $75 per vehicle and Mobile OVI is $199 per vehicle. Diesel motorhomes are $99 for OBD and $229 for OVI. Call (916) 890-4427 to book.

Q: Is Sacramento in the Bay Area?
A: No. Sacramento and Stockton are their own regions. Our Bay Area tester is based at our Oakland hub and can reach Sacramento, Stockton, the North Bay, and down to San Jose. Each area has its own page on this site.

Q: Do you have an Oakland shop I can drive to?
A: No. There is no public Oakland street address and no second Google listing. We come to you. Call (916) 890-4427.
