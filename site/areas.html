What every NorCal visit includes

We come to the yard, office, or jobsite. Engine year on the emission control label picks the test: HD-OBD at $75 for 2013 and newer diesel engines and 2018 and newer alternative-fuel engines; Mobile OVI at $199 for 2012 and older diesels (SAE J1667 smoke opacity plus a visual inspection).
