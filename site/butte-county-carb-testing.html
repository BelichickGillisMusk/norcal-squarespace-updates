Why Butte County is route-priced

Chico and the surrounding towns are a real service route, not a Sacramento-core appointment. Travel time, vehicle count and timing can change the trip substantially.

Three buses at one location may be priced differently from one truck requiring a separate run. We quote the actual job before confirming it.

Before we schedule

Have the city, vehicle count, engine years and deadline ready. For buses or vehicles parked at different locations, tell us the operating window so we can build the route correctly.
