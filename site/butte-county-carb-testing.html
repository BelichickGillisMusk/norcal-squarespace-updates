Getting a Butte County day on the book

Chico is the usual anchor. Oroville, Paradise, Gridley, Biggs, and Durham fill in around that day when the truck counts justify the drive. A single owner-operator in Durham may ride along with a Chico fleet visit; a full bus yard in Oroville can be the reason we run the route that week.

Highway 99 and Highway 70 are the practical spines, not I-880 and not I-80. That is another reason this page should never be glued to Santa Rosa or the Bay Area chip row.

If you also keep trucks in Sacramento, say so. We can often plan the Butte day and a metro stop as related work without pretending they are the same region. Call (916) 890-4427 with both locations.

Official rules, said plainly for this route

Clean Truck Check applies to most non-gasoline heavy-duty vehicles over 14,000 lbs GVWR on California public roads. Engine year on the emission control label picks HD-OBD or Mobile OVI. Almost all subject vehicles test twice a year; agricultural vehicles and recreational CA-registered motorhomes test once a year. Beginning October 2027, OBD-equipped vehicles move to four tests a year while those annual groups stay annual.

We test and submit. CARB issues compliance. That is the same rule on this page as on Sacramento or the Bay — the difference is the drive and the calendar, not the regulation.
