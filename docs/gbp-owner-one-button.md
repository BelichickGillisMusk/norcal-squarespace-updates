## Photos (do this on the phone)

1. Open **Google Business Profile** (app or business.google.com)
2. Tap the NorCal CARB Mobile listing
3. **Photos → Add photos**
4. Upload work-first shots:
   - Branded truck/van
   - On-site testing / equipment
   - Yard or corridor context
5. Skip low-light blur and pure vacation scenery as the main album

Agents cannot see personal camera rolls. Photos must be uploaded from the phone (or dropped in Drive and linked in chat).

## Posts (one approve button)

Ready packs live in `content/approvals/`.

| Pack | Reply with |
|------|------------|
| Mojave GBP + Facebook + blog | `approve mojave` |

That single reply is the only gate. After it, the agent publishes the queued copy (and ships the blog file to the live site folder).

Do **not** use personal names in GBP/Facebook/blog copy. Brand voice: **NorCal CARB Mobile**.

---

## Manual paste (if posting yourself)

1. Business Profile → **Posts** / **Updates** → **Add update**
2. Paste body from the approved pack
3. Add CTA → website → `https://norcalcarbmobile.com/contact`
4. Attach 1–3 photos → Publish
