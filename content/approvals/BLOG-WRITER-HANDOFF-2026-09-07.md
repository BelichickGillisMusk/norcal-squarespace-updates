## Law (confirmed 2026-09-07 on CARB page)

Source: https://ww2.arb.ca.gov/clean-truck-check-emissions-compliance-testing-requirements

- Now through September 2027: most HD diesels over 14,000 lb GVWR = **2 tests per year** (~180 days / +17 weeks).
- Qualifying agricultural vehicles and qualifying CA recreational / emergency motorhomes = **1 test per year**. Annual is not exempt.
- Beginning October 2027: **OBD-equipped** units move to **4 tests per year**. Opacity-path (typically 2012-and-older diesel) stays semi-annual. Ag + those motorhomes stay annual.
- Passing results may be submitted **up to 90 days before** the CTC-VIS deadline.
- Deadline source = CTC-VIS. What stops the truck is a **CA DMV registration hold**.
