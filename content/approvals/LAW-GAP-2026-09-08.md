Posted March 10, 2026 banner: program requirements are in effect for subject vehicles, **including vehicles registered outside of California when operating in the state**, to report, pay an annual compliance fee, and submit required passing emissions tests.

## Cadence

- Most heavy-duty vehicles over 14,000 lb GVWR on California public roads: **two tests per year** now through September 2027.
- Beginning **October 2027**: OBD-equipped vehicles (typically 2013+ diesel / 2018+ alt-fuel) move to **four tests per year**. Opacity-path units stay semi-annual.
- Qualifying **agricultural vehicles** and qualifying **California recreational / emergency motorhomes**: **one test per year**, and they stay annual after October 2027.
- A haul truck moving crop to first point of processing is usually **not** the ag annual exception. Confirm in CTC-VIS.

## Windows that are not the same clock

- **90-day window:** a passing test may be submitted up to 90 days before the CTC-VIS deadline so a fail can be repaired and retested.
- **NST 30-day clock:** if CARB roadside monitoring flags a high emitter, the owner gets a Notice to Submit to Testing. A passing Clean Truck Check must be submitted within **30 calendar days** of receipt. This is separate from the semi-annual calendar.
- **+17 weeks / 4 months:** NorCal ops reminder on the calendar. Not a CARB legal interval. Legal interval is 180 days / CTC-VIS.

## Out-of-state

Out-of-state plates that operate in California are in the program. They report in CTC-VIS, pay the annual fee, and submit passing tests. Deadline table for non-CA / DMV-exempt units is VIN-last-digit (CARB Table II), not the CA DMV month table.
